# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Tilepress to be a welcoming project for everyone who uses it or works on it, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, background, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation. We commit to acting in ways that keep this community open, friendly, diverse and healthy.

## What we expect

Good ways to take part:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Respect that others have different opinions and experiences.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person, and take feedback gracefully.
- Own your mistakes, apologise to the people they affected, and learn from them.
- Think about what is best for the community as a whole, not only for yourself.

Not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Insults, put-downs, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a home or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

This code applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and commit messages. It also applies when someone represents the project elsewhere, for example by posting from an official account or acting as its representative at an event.

## Reporting a problem

If someone's behaviour worries you, tell the maintainer ([epicabdou](https://github.com/epicabdou)) through GitHub:

- **About a specific comment, issue or pull request:** open its **···** menu, choose **Report content**, then **Report to repository admins**. Only the maintainer sees the report.
- **About anything else:** open an issue titled "Code of conduct: please contact me", without any details. The maintainer will reply and arrange a private conversation.

For serious abuse, you can also report the account to GitHub itself with **Report abuse** on the person's profile.

Every report will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The maintainer will respect the privacy and safety of whoever reports an incident.

## How the maintainer responds

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and will explain the decision when it's appropriate.

Depending on how serious the behaviour is and whether it is repeated, the response may be:

1. **Correction.** A private note explaining what was wrong. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour. For a set time, no interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing this code.
3. **Temporary ban.** No interaction or public communication with the community for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a sustained pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression towards or disparagement of groups of people.
